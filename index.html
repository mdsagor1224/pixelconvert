    Frequently asked questions

    What is a JFIF file?

    JFIF (JPEG File Interchange Format) is a container format for JPEG-compressed images. Many programs and some devices save images as .jfif, but not every app can open them.

    How do I convert JFIF to PNG?

    Select or drop your JFIF files, keep PNG as the output format, click Convert Images, then download each file or all as a ZIP.

    Is PixelConvert free?

    Yes. There is no account, subscription or watermark.

    Can I convert multiple images?

    Yes. Add as many files as your device can handle and convert them one after another.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Conversion runs in your browser and image data is not intentionally sent to any server.

    Does converting an image reduce its file size?

    Not necessarily. PNG is lossless and is often larger than JPG or JFIF files.

    Why might a conversion fail?

    The file may be corrupted, not really an image, unsupported by your browser, or too large for your device's memory.

    Can I use PixelConvert on mobile?

    Yes. It works in modern mobile browsers, though very large images may fail on low-memory phones.
