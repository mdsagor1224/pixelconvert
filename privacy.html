Privacy Policy

Images you select are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to any server by PixelConvert.

Third-party resources

The ZIP feature loads the JSZip library from the cdnjs content delivery network (Cloudflare). Loading it may expose your IP address and browser details to that provider. The site is hosted on a static hosting provider (such as Vercel), which may keep standard server logs.

What we do not add

PixelConvert does not include analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels or cookies by default. Any change would be reflected on this page.
